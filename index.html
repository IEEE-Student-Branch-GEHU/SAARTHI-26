ABOUT SAARTHI-26

SAARTHI-26 is the next evolution of Uttarakhand's biggest student-run hackathon—a 36-hour build space where students and innovators from across the country come together to create impactful, real-world tech solutions.


More than just coding, it is a platform to explore ideas, sharpen skills, and grow with a driven community—whether you are just starting out or building complex systems.


Enter SAARTHI-26 and shape the future, one idea at a time.

FAQs

What is SAARTHI-26? ⛏

SAARTHI-26 is a 36-hour hackathon where students and innovators create real-world tech solutions. It's a platform to learn, build, network, and compete for amazing prizes while working on projects that matter.

When and where is SAARTHI-26 taking place? ⛏

SAARTHI-26 dates are TBD. The event location will be announced soon. Stay tuned to our social media channels for updates!

Who can participate in SAARTHI-26? ⛏

SAARTHI-26 is open to all students currently enrolled in any educational institution across India. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced developer, everyone is welcome!

How do I register for the hackathon? ⛏

Click the "Register Now" button on our homepage and fill out the registration form with your details. You'll receive a confirmation email once your registration is complete.

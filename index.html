About SAARTHI-26

SAARTHI-26 is the next edition of Uttarakhand's biggest student-run hackathon: a 36-hour build space where students and innovators from across the country come together to create impactful, real-world tech solutions.

More than just coding, it is a platform to explore ideas, sharpen skills and grow with a driven community, whether you are just starting out or building complex systems.

Enter SAARTHI-26 and shape the future, one idea at a time.

Dates
31 October – 2 November 2026
Venue
Graphic Era Hill University, Dehradun, Uttarakhand
Format
36-hour team hackathon, 8 tracks
Who can apply
Students enrolled in any institution in India
Organised by
Departments of Computer Science & Engineering and Computer Applications, GEHU

Hackathon tracks

Pick the problem space your team wants to work in. Each track is judged on its own.

  • AI, ML & Intelligent Systems

  • Healthcare, Wellness & Assistive Technology

  • Smart Cities, Mobility & Public Safety

  • Sustainability, Climate & Clean Technology

  • Agriculture, Rural Innovation & Food Security

  • Education, FinTech & Digital Inclusion

  • Cybersecurity, Privacy & Emerging Technology

  • Open Innovation

Open Innovation is for teams whose idea doesn't fit the other seven tracks.

Frequently asked questions

What is SAARTHI-26?

SAARTHI-26 is a 36-hour hackathon where students and innovators create real-world tech solutions. It's a platform to learn, build, network and compete for prizes while working on projects that matter.

When and where is SAARTHI-26 taking place?

SAARTHI-26 runs from 31 October to 2 November 2026 (3 days) at Graphic Era Hill University, Dehradun. Follow our social media channels for updates.

Who can participate?

SAARTHI-26 is open to all students currently enrolled in any educational institution across India. Beginners and experienced developers are equally welcome.
